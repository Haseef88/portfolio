Featured work

StyleCue

AI-powered in-store style matching, built for a real retail inventory.

My Role

Backend development & integration - built the backend and database, then worked with my team to connect it to the frontend as lead developer.

01 - The Problem

  • Shoppers are often unsure what to buy or how to match items
  • No stylist is available on the retail floor to help
  • Staff have no way to see customer interest until someone asks

02 - The Idea

An in-store kiosk that gives instant, personalized styling - connected to each store's own real, live inventory, with no account or login needed.

03 - The Product

AI Stylist Quiz

A guided flow - gender, category, occasion, skin tone, body type, measurements - ending in personalized outfit picks.

Browse & Shop

A self-directed path through the store's live catalog, for anyone who'd rather browse than take the quiz.

Live Inventory Matching

Recommendations pulled only from a store's real, current stock - out-of-stock items never show up.

Staff Notifications

A live dashboard alerts staff the moment a customer confirms their picks, so items are ready for the fitting room.

Privacy by Design

Fully anonymous sessions - no accounts, no login, and all data is cleared the moment a visit ends.

Multi-Tenant Architecture

Each store's inventory, orders, and staff logins stay fully isolated from every other store.
